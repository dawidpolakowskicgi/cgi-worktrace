Tip 1 of 8

1. Plan and log

In Today, add up to three outcomes and start a session from one of them. The activity form links the session to that task, even if you change the wording. Pause for breaks, then use the timer time or save directly. The timer adds to any duration already entered and keeps counting while Tavri is closed.

2. Read the history

Activity shows the selected week. List groups entries by day; Timeline places each entry on a dated line and can fill the screen; Contributions shows a year of work. Search archive finds entries across all weekly files. Change history records desktop edits from this version onward.

3. Understand your work

Insights shows saved time by day, project and task, plus the current unsaved timer and a seven-day review. Linked sessions stay together under one task. A short bar means less recorded time, not less progress. Choose a From/To range in Activity to see multiple weeks in the list and Insights. Reports offers presets, comparisons, charts and a data table.

4. Copy or export

Open Export in Activity or Today. Choose Current view or a custom From/To date range, then copy or save TXT, CSV or PDF. Custom ranges can span archived weeks; active search and project filters still apply.

5. Work with a team

In Settings, switch to Admin / PO to reveal Team. Preview a colleague's weekly .log files, folder or ZIP before import. Tavri keeps local snapshots for reports; remove a week or person and undo the last team change when needed.

6. Keep your data safe

In Settings, save a personal backup of weekly logs and the focus plan, or preview one before restoring. Tavri saves current versions to a recovery folder. Team snapshots live separately in .tavri-team. You can also choose an appearance, timeline density and check for published updates manually.

A practical focus loop

Use this cycle for one meaningful piece of work. It keeps the journal useful without constant logging.

  1. Plan. Choose one to three outcomes in Today.
  2. Focus. Start a linked session, pause for breaks and switch to the floating widget if helpful.
  3. Record. Save what changed and the measured duration. Later sessions keep the same task link.
  4. Review. Mark completed outcomes, write a next step or move unfinished work to tomorrow; compare time in Insights.

Habits that make reports better

  • Write the result in one sentence and include a next step when work is unfinished.
  • Link repeated sessions to the same task; old unlinked logs are still grouped by their wording.
  • Pause the timer when switching work. Leave duration empty when you do not know it.
  • Review the longest task bars before planning the next day; they show time spent, not quality or progress.
